Arion Kurtaj, a prominent member of the notorious cybercrime collective Lapsus$, has officially pleaded guilty to charges related to the massive leaks of Grand Theft Auto VI and other major security breaches. The announcement was made by the UK's Crown Prosecution Service (CPS) this week.

At just 21 years old, Kurtaj was the mastermind behind the leak of 90 videos featuring gameplay from the highly anticipated *Grand Theft Auto VI*. These leaks caused significant damage to Rockstar Games' marketing strategy and delayed the official trailer release. Beyond the video game industry, Kurtaj was also prosecuted for hacking British telecommunications giant BT/EE.

According to court reports, the cybercriminal targeted BT/EE between August and November 2021. The attack was sophisticated, involving unauthorized SIM swaps that affected numerous customers. This led to a marked increase in security incidents for the company's mobile users during that period.

The guilty plea marks a significant development in the ongoing fight against organized cybercrime. As developers and companies invest more in digital infrastructure, cases like Kurtaj's serve as a stark reminder of the vulnerabilities that exist within connected systems. The legal proceedings continue as the court determines the final sentencing for these severe offenses.
