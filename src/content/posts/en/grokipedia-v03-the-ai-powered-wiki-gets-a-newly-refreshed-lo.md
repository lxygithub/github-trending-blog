After a period of downtime, **SpaceXAI** has reintroduced edits to **Grokipedia**, its controversial AI-powered alternative to Wikipedia. Following this restoration, the platform has undergone a visual transformation with the release of version 0.3.

The update, dubbed a "newly refreshed" look by Benji Taylor, the head of design at SpaceXAI, introduces several UI improvements. The previous iteration of Grokipedia was notably minimalist, featuring a simple logo and a search bar. The new homepage, however, is significantly more feature-rich.

Users can now access a list of featured articles, view the most-read articles, and utilize a new tracker. These additions suggest a move toward a more engaging and information-dense user experience. As the project continues to develop, developers and tech enthusiasts will be watching closely to see how Grokipedia evolves in the coming updates.
