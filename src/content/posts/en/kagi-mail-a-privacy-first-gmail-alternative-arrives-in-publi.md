Kagi, the company behind the privacy-focused Orion browser and a popular alternative to Google Search, is expanding its ecosystem with a new offering. Today, the company announced the public beta launch of Kagi Mail, a direct competitor to Google’s ubiquitous Gmail service.

For developers and privacy advocates who have grown wary of data harvesting and intrusive advertising, Kagi Mail positions itself as a breath of fresh air. The service is built on a philosophy of "no surveillance, no ads, open standards, and no lock-in." This commitment to user sovereignty is a significant draw for those looking to break free from corporate ecosystems.

**Key Features of Kagi Mail**
Kagi Mail aims to provide a robust experience that rivals established players while maintaining a strict privacy mandate. The service is currently available as a public beta, offering the following core features:

*   **Custom Domain:** Users receive a standard @kagimail.com email address.
*   **Flexibility:** The service supports unlimited email aliases and custom domains, allowing users to manage multiple identities without clutter.
*   **Storage:** It offers 50GB of storage, ensuring ample space for attachments and archives.
*   **Integrated Tools:** A built-in calendar is included to manage schedules directly within the inbox.

**Why Developers Should Care**
While the features are impressive, the technical implementation is what makes this news relevant to the developer community. By adhering to "open standards," Kagi Mail ensures that data is not trapped within a proprietary walled garden. Users can easily migrate their data or interact with the email service using standard protocols.

The launch of Kagi Mail complements Kagi's existing suite of developer-friendly tools. For those already using the Kagi search engine or the Orion browser, a seamless transition to Kagi Mail offers a consistent, privacy-respecting workflow. As the beta phase begins, it will be interesting to watch how Kagi further develops its roadmap, particularly regarding contacts management and additional integrations.

For those seeking a cleaner, ad-free email experience, Kagi Mail presents a compelling option to reconsider.
